# Impact Proof: privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Impact Proof is a demo app built for the Meta Global AI Developer Hackathon. It is not a commercial service and is not used by any nonprofit. It only uses staged test data.

## Who runs it

Panagiotis (Panos) Kokmotos, an individual developer. Contact: panagiotis.kokmotoss@gmail.com

## What data it handles

The app only accepts WhatsApp messages from phone numbers the developer has added to an allowlist (currently only the developer's own test phone). Messages from any other number are ignored and not stored.

For allowlisted numbers it stores:

- the phone number and the message ID
- the text, photo or voice note sent
- a transcript of voice notes, and the facts drafted from the message (item, quantity, unit, place, time, delivery status)
- the reviewer's confirmations and corrections, with timestamps

## How it is used

- Text, photos and voice notes are sent to the Meta Model API to transcribe voice notes and draft the facts. While the developer's Meta account uses free credits, Meta may use that content to improve its products.
- A signed-in reviewer checks the drafted facts. A donor report is built only from confirmed facts and never includes phone numbers, photos, transcripts or exact addresses.
- Data is not sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

## Where it is stored and for how long

Data is stored on the developer's own computer. Each case, with its photos, voice notes and transcripts, is deleted automatically 14 days after it was created.

## Data deletion

To have your data deleted sooner, email panagiotis.kokmotoss@gmail.com and include the phone number you messaged from. Your data will be deleted within 7 days and you will get a confirmation.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change.
